Let $\mathcal{A}' = \{A \in \mathcal{A} \colon d(v^*, D(A)) \ge \theta'\}$.

\hspace*{12pt}(13) \textit{$\mathcal{A}'$ is adequate for $\phi'$.}

\textit{Subproof.} Let us verify the four conditions in the definition of ``adequate''. For (i), let $v \in V(H)$ and $A \in \mathcal{A} \setminus \mathcal{A}'$; we must show that $A \cap \phi'(v) \subseteq \Gamma \cup N_W$. Now trivially $A \cap \Gamma \subseteq \Gamma \cup N_W$. Since $A \notin \mathcal{A}'$ it follows from 7.1(iii) that $A \cap K_1$ is null. For $1 \le i \le t$, $A \ne A'_i$ since $A \notin \mathcal{A}'$, and so $A \cap A'_i \subseteq \Gamma \cup N_W$ by (J2) and (3). Consequently,
\begin{equation*}
A \cap \phi'(v) \subseteq A \cap (\Gamma \cup K_1 \cup A'_1 \cup \cdots \cup A'_t) \subseteq \Gamma \cup N_W.
\end{equation*}
This proves (i).

For (ii), let $v \in V(H)$. Now
\begin{equation*}
A'_1 \cup \cdots \cup A'_{t+1} \subseteq K \cup \bigcup(A \colon A \in \mathcal{A}'),
\end{equation*}
since $A'_1, \ldots, A'_t \in \mathcal{A}'$ and $A'_{t+1} \subseteq K_1 \subseteq K \cup \bigcup(A \colon A \in \mathcal{A}')$. But by (12), $\phi'(v) \cap (A'_1 \cup \cdots \cup A'_{t+1})$ is non-null, and (ii) follows.

For (iii), let $e \in E(H)$. By (12),
\begin{equation*}
\phi'(e) \in E(A'_1 \cup \cdots \cup A'_{t+1}) \subseteq E\Bigl(K \cup \bigcup(A \colon A \in \mathcal{A}')\Bigr).
\end{equation*}
This proves (iii).

For (iv), let $A \in \mathcal{A}$ with $A \cap K \ne N_W$. By (J5), $d(v^*, D(A)) \ge \theta$, and so $A \in \mathcal{A}'$. This proves (iv), and hence proves (13).

From (13), hypotheses (iii) and 7.2, the result follows. $\square$

Now we need to relax the definition of ``adequate'' a little. If (J1)--(J6) hold and $\mathcal{A}' \subseteq \mathcal{A}$, and $\phi'$ is a model of $(H, \chi)$ in $(G, \omega)$, we say that $\mathcal{A}'$ is \textit{sufficient} for $\phi'$ if

\hspace*{12pt}$\bullet$ for each $v \in V(H)$ and each $A \in \mathcal{A}$, if some edge of $A \cap \phi'(v)$ is incident with a vertex in $W$ then $A \in \mathcal{A}'$,

\hspace*{12pt}$\bullet$ for each $v \in V(H)$, $\phi'(v) \cap (K \cup \bigcup(A \colon A \in \mathcal{A}'))$ is non-null,

\hspace*{12pt}$\bullet$ for each $e \in E(H)$, $\phi'(e) \subseteq E(K \cup \bigcup(A \colon A \in \mathcal{A}'))$, and

\hspace*{12pt}$\bullet$ for each $A \in \mathcal{A}$, if $A \cap K \ne N_W$ then $A \in \mathcal{A}'$.

Thus, if $\mathcal{A}'$ is adequate for $\phi'$ then it is sufficient for $\phi'$.
