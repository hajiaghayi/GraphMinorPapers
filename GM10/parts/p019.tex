\sectionhead{7. A Tangle in a Grid}

Let $\theta \ge 2$ be an integer. Let $G$ be a simple graph with $V(G) = \{(i, j) : 1 \le i, j \le \theta\}$, where $(i, j)$ and $(i', j')$ are adjacent if $|i' - i| + |j' - j| = 1$. We call $G$ a \textit{$\theta$-grid}. The object of this section is to prove the existence of a natural tangle of order $\theta$ in a $\theta$-grid.
