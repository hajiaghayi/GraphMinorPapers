\sectionhead{8. Stars with Small Hearts}

The results of the previous two sections will be used to show that our containment relation satisfies Axioms 1, 2, and 3. In this section we prove a result will be used for Axiom 4. We begin with the following lemma.

Let $P = (G, \mu, \Delta, \phi)$ be an $\Omega$-patchwork. If $G'$ is a rooted hypergraph with $G'^- \subseteq G^-$, and $\mu'$, $\Delta'$, $\phi'$ are the restrictions of $\mu$, $\Delta$, $\phi$ to $\operatorname{dom}(\mu) \cap E(G')$, $E(G')$, $E(G')$, respectively, then $(G', \mu', \Delta', \phi')$ is an $\Omega$-patchwork which we denote by $P | G'$.

\stmt
(8.1) \textit{Let $P = (G, \mu, \Delta, \phi)$, $P' = (G', \mu', \Delta', \phi')$ be $\Omega$-patchworks, and let $(G_1, G_2)$, $(G'_1, G'_2)$ be separations of $G$, $G'$ respectively. Let $\eta_1$ be a realizable expansion of $P | G_1$ in $P' | G'_1$ (whence $|\bar{\pi}(G_1)| = |\bar{\pi}(G'_1)| = k$, say) and let $\eta_2$ be a realizable expansion of $P | G_2$ in $P' | G'_2$ such that for $1 \le i \le k$, $\eta_2(v)$ contains the $i$th term of $\pi(G'_1)$, where $v$ is the $i$th term of $\pi(G_1)$. Define $\eta$ by}
\begin{align*}
\eta(v) &= \eta_1(v) && (v \in V(G_1) - V(G_2)) \\
&= \eta_2(v) && (v \in V(G_2) - V(G_1)) \\
&= \eta_1(v) \cup \eta_2(v) && (v \in V(G_1) \cap V(G_2)) \\
\eta(e) &= \eta_1(e) && (e \in E(G_1)) \\
&= \eta_2(e) && (e \in E(G_2)).
\end{align*}
\textit{Then $\eta$ is a realizable expansion of $P$ in $P'$.}
