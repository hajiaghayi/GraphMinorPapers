\textit{Subproof.} Now
\begin{align*}
\theta - \chi &\le d'(y_1, y_2) \le d'(y_1, p(y_1)) + d'(p(y_1), p(y_2)) + d'(p(y_2), y_2) \\
&\le 2\gamma + d'(p(y_1), p(y_2))
\end{align*}
since for $i = 1, 2, \Lambda_{y_i}$ is a $\gamma$-zone around $y_i$ and $p(y_i) \in \bar{\Lambda}_{y_i}$. But
\begin{equation*}
d'(p(y_1), p(y_2)) \le d''(p(y_1), p(y_2)) + (4\gamma + 2)\delta
\end{equation*}
since $\mathscr{T}''$ is a $(4\gamma + 2)\delta$-compression of $\mathscr{T}'$. Consequently,
\begin{equation*}
d''(p(y_1), p(y_2)) \ge \theta - \chi - 2\gamma - (4\gamma + 2)\delta \ge \theta_2.
\end{equation*}
This proves (5).

\hspace*{12pt}(6) \textit{For each $y \in Y$ there is a $H''$-path in $G \setminus X$ with ends $p(y), q(y)$ say, such that $d''(p(y), q(y)) \ge \theta_2$.}

\textit{Subproof.} Let $Q_y$ be an $H$-path in $G \setminus X$ with ends $y, r(y)$ say, such that $d'(y, r(y)) \ge \theta - \chi$. Let $R_y$ be a minimal path of $H$ between $r(y)$ and $V(H')$, with ends $r(y), q(y)$ say. Then for some $y' \in Y, \bar{\Lambda}_{y'}$ contains both $r(y)$ and $q(y)$, and so
\begin{equation*}
d'(y', r(y)), d'(y', q(y)) \le \gamma.
\end{equation*}
Hence $d'(q(y), r(y)) \le 2\gamma$. But $d'(y, p(y)) \le \gamma$ since $p(y) \in \bar{\Lambda}_y$, and so
\begin{align*}
\theta - \chi &\le d'(y, r(y)) \le d'(y, p(y)) + d'(p(y), q(y)) + d'(q(y), r(y)) \\
&\le 3\gamma + d'(p(y), q(y)).
\end{align*}
Thus $d'(p(y), q(y)) \ge \theta - \chi - 3\gamma \ge \theta_2$, and so $P_y \cup Q_y \cup R_y$ is an $H''$-path satisfying (6). This proves (6).

Let $Y'' = \{p(y) \colon y \in Y\}$. From (4)--(6) it follows that $(H'', \mathscr{T}'', X, Y'')$ is an animal in $G$ with respect to $\mathscr{T}^*$ with $\chi$ horns and $\delta$ hairs, of strength $\ge (\theta_2, \zeta + \tau)$. By 8.3 (with $\theta, H, \mathscr{T}, Y, Z$ replaced by $\theta_2, H'', \mathscr{T}'', Y'', Z \cup X$) we deduce that one of the outcomes of 8.3 holds.

If the first outcome of 8.3 holds, then 7.1.3 is true. Suppose that the second outcome of 8.3 holds, that is, there is an $H''$-path $P$ in $G \setminus (X \cup Z)$ with ends $s_1, s_2$ and there are distinct $y_1, y_2 \in Y''$ with $d''(s_1, y_1), d''(s_2, y_2) < \psi$. Then $P$ is an $H$-path in $G \setminus X$, not meeting $Z$, and so $d''(s_1, s_2) < 2\psi$ by (3). Hence
\begin{equation*}
d''(y_1, y_2) \le d''(y_1, s_1) + d''(s_1, s_2) + d'(s_2, y_2) < 4\psi \le \theta_2
\end{equation*}
contrary to (5). Thus this case does not occur. We may therefore assume that the third outcome of 8.3 holds, that is, there is a $(\lambda, 2\psi)$-level $\Sigma$-span $H'', \mathscr{T}_0$ of order $\theta_2 - |X \cup Z|$ in $G \setminus (X \cup Z)$ with respect to $\mathscr{T}^* \setminus (X \cup Z)$. But then 7.1.3 holds, since $\theta_2 - |X \cup Z| \ge \theta_2 - \zeta \ge \theta'$. In all cases, therefore, 7.1 holds, as required. $\square$
