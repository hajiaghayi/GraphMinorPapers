\textbf{5.5.} \textit{For any surface $\Sigma$ and integer $p \ge 0$, there exists $\theta \ge 1$ such that, if $\mathscr{T}^*$ is a tangle in a graph $G$ and there is a hairless animal with $\frac{1}{2}p(p - 1)$ horns and with strength $\ge (\theta, 4p(p - 1))$, then $\mathscr{T}^*$ controls a $K_p$-minor of $G$.}
