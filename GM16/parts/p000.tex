\sectionhead{\textbf{About this copy}}

This is a corrected copy of the paper cited above. The text is retyped from the printed paper.
It keeps the paper's wording and the numbering of its results, equations and footnotes, and a
marker such as \origpagemark{$N$} shows where page $N$ of the printed paper begins.

The corrections come from a formalization of the Graph Minors series in the Lean proof
assistant, \href{https://github.com/mahdi-jfri/GraphMinor}{github.com/mahdi-jfri/GraphMinor},
in which the results of this paper are proved. Where the formalization found that a statement
or a step of a proof is wrong as printed, this copy corrects it in place. Where the paper leaves a proof or a step to the reader, this
copy adds it, also in blue. Text \del{struck
through in red} is removed and text \fix{in blue} is inserted. Each change has a note, marked
\textcolor{fixcolor}{\sffamily F1}, \textcolor{fixcolor}{\sffamily F2}, \dots, that gives the
reason. The file
\href{https://github.com/mahdi-jfri/GraphMinor/blob/main/corrections/GM16.md}{corrections/GM16.md}
of the formalization describes each correction in full. Each note, and each item in the list below, begins with the kind of the change. A \kindname{misprint} is a wrong symbol, index or word. A \kindname{statement} is a result or a claim of the text that is false as printed. A \kindname{degenerate} is a statement that fails only in a trivial case, such as an empty graph or a parameter equal to~$0$. A \kindname{gap} is a step of a proof that does not follow as written. A \kindname{missing} is a proof that the paper omits, leaves to the reader or only sketches, and that this copy adds.

The corrections and added proofs, in the order of the pages:
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
\item \kindtag{fix:r-ring}\hyperref[fix:r-ring]{Section 1, page 45}: in the definition of an $r$-ring,
$t_i \in X_1$ becomes $t_i \in X_i$.
\item \kindtag{fix:design}\hyperref[fix:design]{Section 2, page 47}: in the definition of a design, ``in'' becomes
``is''.
\item \kindtag{fix:2.2-proof-added}\hyperref[fix:2.2-proof-added]{Proof of 2.2, page 48}: the proof is added.
\item \kindtag{fix:2-torso}\hyperref[fix:2-torso]{Section 2, page 48}: the sentence before 2.3 is restricted to the
case $Z \cap V(H) = \emptyset$, and a weaker form is added for the general case.
\item \kindtag{fix:2.3}\hyperref[fix:2.3]{Statement of 2.3, page 48}: the second case is weakened in the same
way.
\item \kindtag{fix:1.3-Cij}\hyperref[fix:1.3-Cij]{Proof of 1.3, page 51}: $B$ becomes $B_i^j$ in the definition of
$C_i^j$.
\item \kindtag{fix:1.3-location-proof-added}\hyperref[fix:1.3-location-proof-added]{Proof of 1.3, page 51}: the proof that
$\mathcal{L}$ is a location whose design belongs to $\mathcal{D}$ is added.
\item \kindtag{fix:1.3-location}\hyperref[fix:1.3-location]{Proof of 1.3, page 51}: a separation of order at most 2 is
added to $\mathcal{L}^+$ for each edge with an end in $Z$ and all its ends in $Z$ or in the core
of $\mathcal{L}$.
\item \kindtag{fix:1.3-proof}\hyperref[fix:1.3-proof]{Proof of 1.3, page 52}: the last step uses the weaker forms of
page 48, and $r$-rings become $(w + 1)r$-rings.
\item \kindtag{fix:3.1-proof}\hyperref[fix:3.1-proof]{Proof of 3.1, page 54}: $i \le j \le n$ becomes $i < j \le n$.
\item \kindtag{fix:5.5-proof}\hyperref[fix:5.5-proof]{Proof of 5.5, page 57}: the letter l becomes the digit 1.
\item \kindtag{fix:6.3-animal}\hyperref[fix:6.3-animal]{Proof of 6.3, page 60}: $X \cup \{x\}$ becomes
$X \cup \{v\}$.
\item \kindtag{fix:6.3-Y2}\hyperref[fix:6.3-Y2]{Proof of 6.3, page 60}: $y \in Y_2$ becomes
$y \in (Y \setminus Y_1) \setminus Y_2$.
\item \kindtag{fix:6.3-zone}\hyperref[fix:6.3-zone]{Proof of 6.3, page 60}: $d(z, x)$ becomes $d(\{w\}, x)$.
\item \kindtag{fix:6.3-far-ends}\hyperref[fix:6.3-far-ends]{Proof of 6.3, page 60}: $Y_4$ becomes
$\{v(y) : y \in Y_4\}$, twice.
\item \kindtag{fix:6.3-span}\hyperref[fix:6.3-span]{Proof of 6.3, page 60}: $\theta - 1$ becomes $\theta_1$.
\item \kindtag{fix:6.3-dist}\hyperref[fix:6.3-dist]{Proof of 6.3, page 61}: $d(y, v(y)) = \theta_1$ becomes
$d(y, v(y)) \ge \theta$.
\item \kindtag{fix:6.3-compression}\hyperref[fix:6.3-compression]{Proof of 6.3, page 61}: $=$ becomes $\ge$, twice.
\item \kindtag{fix:5.1-proof}\hyperref[fix:5.1-proof]{Proof of 5.1, page 61}: $\theta \le i \le \delta$ becomes
$0 \le i \le \delta$.
\item \kindtag{fix:8.3-proof}\hyperref[fix:8.3-proof]{Proof of 8.3, page 67}: $|Y'|$ becomes $|Y_1|$.
\item \kindtag{fix:8.1-p71}\hyperref[fix:8.1-p71]{Proof of 8.1, page 71}: $d(y_1, \sigma)$ becomes
$d(y_i, \sigma)$.
\item \kindtag{fix:8.1-p73}\hyperref[fix:8.1-p73]{Proof of 8.1, page 73}: $r \cap U(C) \notin \emptyset$ becomes
$r \cap U(C) \ne \emptyset$.
\item \kindtag{fix:8.1-p74}\hyperref[fix:8.1-p74]{Proof of 8.1, page 74}: ``which'' becomes ``with'', and
$s_2 \notin s_1$ becomes $s_2 \ne s_1$.
\item \kindtag{fix:8.1-p75a}\hyperref[fix:8.1-p75a]{Proof of 8.1, page 75}: $\ge$ becomes $\le$ in a triangle
inequality, and $d(v, y_1)$ becomes $d(u, y_1)$.
\item \kindtag{fix:8.1-p75b}\hyperref[fix:8.1-p75b]{Proof of 8.1, page 75}: $d(u, y_j) \ge$ becomes
$d(u, y_1) \le$ in a triangle inequality.
\end{enumerate}
