Our second lemma is the following.

\textbf{8.3.} \textit{Let $\Sigma$ be a surface, and let $\tau, \chi, \delta, \lambda, \zeta \ge 0$ and $\psi \ge 3$. Then there exists $\theta > \zeta$ such that if $\mathcal{T}^*$ is a tangle in a graph $G$, and $(H, \mathcal{T}, X, Y)$ is an animal with $\chi$ horns and $\delta$ hairs, of strength $\ge (\theta, \zeta + \tau)$, and $Z \subseteq V(G) \setminus V(H)$ with $X \subseteq Z$ and $|Z| \le \zeta$, then either:}

\hspace*{12pt}$\bullet$ \textit{there is an animal with $\chi$ horns and $\delta + 1$ hairs of strength $\ge (\psi, \tau)$, or}

\hspace*{12pt}$\bullet$ \textit{there is an $H$-path in $G \setminus Z$ with ends $s_1, s_2$ and there are distinct $y_1, y_2 \in Y$ with $d(s_1, y_1), d(s_2, y_2) < \psi$, where $d$ is the metric of $\mathcal{T}$, or}

\hspace*{12pt}$\bullet$ \textit{$H, \mathcal{T}_0$ is a $(\lambda, 2\psi)$-level $\Sigma$-span of order $\theta - |Z|$ in $G \setminus Z$ with respect to $\mathcal{T}^* \setminus Z$, where $\mathcal{T}_0$ is the truncation of $\mathcal{T}$ to order $\theta - |Z|$.}

\textbf{Proof.} Let $\theta = 4\psi + 10\lambda + 3\zeta + 11$. We claim that $\theta$ satisfies 8.3. For let $\mathcal{T}^*, G, H, \mathcal{T}, X, Y, Z$ be as in the hypothesis of 8.3, and let $d$ be the metric of $\mathcal{T}$. We may assume that

\hspace*{12pt}(1) $ord(\mathcal{T}) = \theta$.

\textit{Subproof.} Let $\mathcal{T}'$ be the truncation of $\mathcal{T}$ to order $\theta$. Then $(H, \mathcal{T}', X, Y)$ is another animal satisfying the same hypotheses, and since $\theta \ge \psi$, it is easy to see that if the theorem is true for $(H, \mathcal{T}', X, Y)$ then it is true for $(H, \mathcal{T}, X, Y)$. Thus it suffices to prove the theorem for $(H, \mathcal{T}', X, Y)$ and (1) follows.

Also, we may assume that

\hspace*{12pt}(2) \textit{If there is an $H$-path in $G \setminus Z$ with ends $s_1, s_2$ then $d(s_1, s_2) < 2\psi$.}

\textit{Subproof.} Suppose that $d(s_1, s_2) \ge 2\psi$. If $d(s_1, y) \ge \psi$ for all $y \in Y$, then $(H, \mathcal{T}, X, Y \cup \{s_1\})$ is an animal with $\chi$ horns and $\delta + 1$ hairs of strength $\ge (\psi, \tau)$, since $X \subseteq Z$, and so the first outcome of 8.3 holds. We may assume then that $d(s_1, y_1) < \psi$ for some $y_1 \in Y$, and similarly $d(s_2, y_2) < \psi$ for some $y_2 \in Y$. But $y_1 \ne y_2$ since
\begin{equation*}
2\psi \le d(s_1, s_2) \le d(s_1, y_1) + d(y_1, y_2) + d(y_2, s_2) < 2\psi + d(y_1, y_2)
\end{equation*}
and so the second outcome of 8.3 holds. Thus we may assume (2).

Let $\mathcal{T}_0$ be the truncation of $\mathcal{T}$ to order $\theta - |Z|$.
