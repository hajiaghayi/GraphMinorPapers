\stmt
(7.1) \textit{There is a $(\lambda, \mu)$-flat $\Sigma$-span of order $\theta$, with $k$ eyes mutually at distance $\theta$.}

\textit{Proof.} Let $H, \mathcal{T}$ be a $(\lambda, \mu)$-flat $\Sigma$-span of order $\ge \theta$ with $k$ eyes $r_1, \ldots, r_k$ satisfying $d(r_i, r_j) \ge \theta$ ($1 \le i < j \le k$). Let $\mathcal{T}'$ be the $\theta$-truncation of $\mathcal{T}$. Then $H, \mathcal{T}'$ is a $\Sigma$-span, and it is $(\lambda, \mu)$-flat by (5.1), since $\theta \ge 4\lambda + \mu + 2$. Moreover, $r_1, \ldots, r_k$ are eyes of $H, \mathcal{T}'$ since $\operatorname{ord}(\mathcal{T}') = \theta \ge 4$; and for $1 \le i < j \le k$, $d'(r_i, r_j) = \min(\theta, d(r_i, r_j)) = \theta$. The result follows. $\square$
