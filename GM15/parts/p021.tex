\hspace*{12pt}(9) \textit{Every component of $\eta'(M_1)$ meets $\eta(L_1)$, and every component of $\eta''(M_2)$ meets $\eta'(L_2) = \eta(L_2)$.}

\textit{Subproof.} Let $C$ be a component of $M_1$ (so $\eta'(C)$ is a component of $\eta'(M_1)$). Now $M_1 \ne H'$ since $L_2$ is not null by (7), and since $H'$ is 2-cell and hence connected and $Z_1 \cap Z_2 = \emptyset$, there is a vertex $x \in V(M_1)$ incident with an edge $e$ of $H'$ not in $E(M_1)$, and with its other end not in $V(M_1)$. Then $e \in E(H)$, and so $x \in V(H)$, and since $x \in Z_1$ it follows that $x \in V(L_1)$. Moreover, $\eta(x) = \eta'(x)$ (because $\eta(e) = \eta'(e)$ and $\eta(y) = \eta'(y)$ where $e$ has ends $x, y$). Hence $\eta(x) \in V(\eta'(C)) \cap V(\eta(L_1))$, and so $\eta'(C)$ meets $\eta(L_1)$, as required. The argument for $\eta''(M_2)$ is similar. This proves (9).

Let $X_1$ be the union of $V(\eta(L_1)) \cup V(\eta'(M_1))$ with the set

Let $X_2$ be the union of $V(\eta(L_2)) \cup V(\eta''(M_2))$ with
\begin{equation*}
\{\eta(x) : x \in V(H) \text{ and } d(\sigma_2, x) < \mu \text{ for some } \sigma_2 \in Z_2\}.
\end{equation*}

\hspace*{12pt}(10) \textit{Every path of $G$ from $X_1$ to $X_2$ has an internal vertex or edge in $\eta(H)$.}

\textit{Subproof.} Suppose that some path of $G$ from $X_1$ to $X_2$ has no internal vertex or edge in $\eta(H)$. By (9), there is an $\eta(H)$-path with ends $\eta(t_1), \eta(t_2)$ where $d(\sigma_i, t_i) \le \mu$ for some $\sigma_i \in Z_i$ ($i = 1, 2$). Since $H, \eta, \mathcal{T}$ is $(0, \mu)$-flat by (5.1), it follows that $d(t_1, t_2) < \mu$, and so $d(\sigma_1, \sigma_2) < 3\mu \le \lambda - 2\lambda_1 - \lambda_2$ contrary to (6). This proves (10).

It follows from (10) that $\eta(L_1)$ is disjoint from $\eta''(M_2)$. For each vertex or edge $x$ of $H_1$, define $\eta'''(x) = \eta(x)$ unless $x$ belongs to $M_2$, when $\eta'''(x) = \eta''(x)$. Then $\eta'''$ is an isomorphism from $H'''$ to the subgraph $\eta(H_0) \cup \eta(L_1) \cup \eta''(M_2)$ of $G$.
