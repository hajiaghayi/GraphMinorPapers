\hspace*{12pt}(4) \textit{$r_2, \dots, r_k$ are eyes of $H', \eta', \mathcal{T}'$.}

\textit{Subproof.} Let $2 \le i \le k$. Since $r_i$ is an eye of $H \cap (\Sigma - \Lambda_1), \mathcal{T}_1$, there are two disjoint $H \cap (\Sigma - \Lambda_1)$-paths $P, Q$ with ends in $\text{bd}(r_i)$ in alternating order. We claim that $P, Q$ are $\eta'(H')$-paths. For if $P$ is not an $\eta'(H')$-path, then $P$ meets one of $P_1, \dots, P_{\tau - 4}$, and so there is an $H \cap (\Sigma - \Lambda_1)$-path in $G$ with one end, $u$, in $\text{bd}(r_i)$ and the other, $v$, in $\text{bd}(\Lambda_1)$. Since
\begin{equation*}
d_1(u, v) \ge d_1(r_i, v) - 1 \ge d(r_i, v) - (12\beta + 14) - 1
\end{equation*}
and
\begin{equation*}
d(r_i, v) \ge d(r_i, r_1) - d(r_1, v) = \theta - d(r_1, v) \ge \theta - (3\beta + 3)
\end{equation*}
it follows that $d_1(u, v) \ge \theta - 15\beta - 18 \ge \mu$, and so $H \cap (\Sigma - \Lambda_1), \mathcal{T}_1$ is $\mu$-stepped. But $H \cap (\Sigma - \Lambda_1), \mathcal{T}_1$ is obtained from $H, \mathcal{T}$ by rearranging within $3\beta + 3$ of $r_1$, and $3\beta + 3 \le \lambda$, and $H, \mathcal{T}$ is $(\lambda, \mu)$-flat, a contradiction. This proves that $P$ and similarly $Q$ are $\eta'(H')$-paths, and hence proves (4).

\hspace*{12pt}(5) \textit{For $2 \le i < j \le k$, $d'(r_i, r_j) \ge \theta'$.}

\textit{Subproof.} $d'(r_i, r_j) \ge d(r_i, r_j) - 12\beta - 14 = \theta - 12\beta - 14 \ge \theta'$.

\hspace*{12pt}(6) \textit{For $2 \le i \le k$, $d'(s_1, r_i) \ge \theta'$ and $d'(s_2, r_i) \ge \theta'$.}

\textit{Subproof.} $d'(s_1, r_i) \ge d_1(\Lambda_1, r_i) \ge d_1(r_1, r_i) - 12\beta - 14 = \theta - 12\beta - 14 \ge \theta'$. Similarly $d'(s_2, r_i) \ge \theta'$.

\hspace*{12pt}(7) \textit{$H', \eta', \mathcal{T}'$ is $(\lambda', \mu)$-flat.}

\textit{Subproof.} Now $3\beta + 3, \lambda' \ge 4$ and $\mu \ge 1$, and $\lambda \ge 6(3\beta + 3) + 5\lambda' + 3\mu + 4$. Since $H', \eta', \mathcal{T}'$ is obtained from $H, \mathcal{T}$ by rearranging within $3\beta + 3$ of $r_1$, and $H, \mathcal{T}$ is $(\lambda, \mu)$-flat, the result follows from (5.2). This proves (7).
