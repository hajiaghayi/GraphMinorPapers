\sectionhead{6 Presentations}

If $\Gamma$ is a drawing in a surface $\Sigma$ such that $|V(\Gamma) \cap C| \ge 2$ for every cuff $C$, we call the connected components of $bd(\Sigma) \setminus V(\Gamma)$ the \textit{spaces} of $\Gamma$ in $\Sigma$. For every space $s$ there are two vertices $u, v$ such that $s \cup \{u, v\}$ is a line; we call $u, v$ the \textit{ends} of $s$. A \textit{support} of $\Gamma$ in $\Sigma$ is either a set $\{v\}$ where $v \in V(\Gamma) \cap bd(\Sigma)$, or a line $F \subseteq bd(\Sigma)$ with both ends in $V(\Gamma)$ (where possibly some internal points of $F$ belong to $V(\Gamma)$). The \textit{ends} of a support $F$ are $a$ and $b$, where $a = b = v$ if $F = \{v\}$, and $a$ and $b$ are the ends of the line $F$ if $F$ is a line.

Let $\Gamma$, as above, be a subgraph of some graph $G$, which is not required to be a drawing. Let $F_1^*, \dots, F_r^*$ be mutually disjoint supports of $\Gamma$ in $\Sigma$ with $V(\Gamma) \cap bd(\Sigma) \subseteq F_1^* \cup \dots \cup F_r^*$. For $1 \le j \le r$, let $H_j \subseteq G$, so that

\hspace*{12pt}(V1) $H_1 \cup \dots \cup H_r \cup \Gamma = G$; $H_1, \dots, H_r$ \textit{are mutually vertex-disjoint; and for} $1 \le j \le r, V(H_j \cap \Gamma) = V(\Gamma) \cap F_j^*$, \textit{and} $E(H_j \cap \Gamma) = \emptyset$.
