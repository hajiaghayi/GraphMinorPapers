The main result of this section is the following.

\textbf{5.2} \textit{For every surface} $\Sigma$ \textit{with} $bd(\Sigma) = \emptyset$ \textit{and every integer} $p \ge 0$ \textit{there exists} $\theta \ge 1$ \textit{such that every drawing in} $\Sigma$ \textit{with a tangle of order} $\ge \theta$ \textit{has no vital} $p$\textit{-linkage.}

\textbf{Proof.} Let $\Sigma_0$ be a surface with $bd(\Sigma_0) = \emptyset$, and assume that the result holds for all pairs $\Sigma', p'$ where $\Sigma'$ is simpler than $\Sigma_0$. We shall prove that it holds for $\Sigma_0$ and all $p$. By cutting at most $p$ small holes in $\Sigma_0$, one at each terminal, we deduce that it suffices to prove the following.

(\textit{) *For every surface} $\Sigma$ \textit{with} $\hat{\Sigma}$ \textit{homeomorphic to} $\Sigma_0$ \textit{and every integer} $p \ge 0$, \textit{there exists} $\theta \ge 1$ \textit{such that every drawing} $\Gamma$ \textit{in} $\Sigma$ \textit{with} $|V(\Gamma) \cap bd(\Sigma)| \le p$ \textit{and with a tangle of order} $\ge \theta$ \textit{has no vital linkage with all its terminals in} $bd(\Sigma)$.

We shall prove (*) for all $p$ by induction on $c(\Sigma)$, and then, with $c(\Sigma)$ fixed, by induction on $p$. Our three inductive hypothesis may be summarized as follows.

\hspace*{12pt}(1) \textit{For all} $p' \ge 0$ \textit{there exists} $\theta_1(p') \ge 1$ \textit{such that for every surface} $\Sigma'$ \textit{with} $bd(\Sigma') = \emptyset$ \textit{which is simpler than} $\Sigma_0$, \textit{every drawing in} $\Sigma'$ \textit{with a tangle of order} $\ge \theta_1(p')$ \textit{has no vital} $p'$\textit{-linkage.}
