\noindent that $(A \cap H, B \cap H) \in \mathscr{T}$. Consequently, $\mathscr{S}' = \mathscr{S}_2 \cup \mathscr{S}^*$ is a $\mathscr{T}$-local 3-segregation of $G$. For $v \in V(\mathscr{S}')$, define $\alpha'(v) = \alpha(v)$ if $v \in V(\mathscr{S}_2)$, and $\alpha'(v) = \alpha^*(v)$ otherwise. For $(A, \Omega) \in \mathscr{S}'$, define $\alpha'(A, \Omega) = \alpha(A, \Omega)$ if $(A, \Omega) \in \mathscr{S}_2$, and $\alpha'(A, \Omega) = \alpha^*(A, \Omega)$ otherwise. Then $\alpha'$ is an arrangement of $\mathscr{S}'$ in $\Sigma$ compatible with $H$, as required. $\square$
