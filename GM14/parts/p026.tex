Also, since $(\Lambda, C, X)$ is a battlefield, it follows that $x \subseteq \Lambda \subseteq \Delta - bd(\Delta)$ for every $x \in A(H)$ with $d(z, x) < \beta$. Thus, $\Delta$ and $\Lambda$ satisfy (i), (ii) and (iii), and it remains to verify (iv). Suppose that (iv)(b) is false; we shall show that (iv)(a) is true. Thus, $(G^*, \alpha^{-1}(bd(\Delta)))$ has a crooked transaction of cardinality $\ge \pi$, where $G^* = \alpha^{-1}(\Delta) \cup J$.
