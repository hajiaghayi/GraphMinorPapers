\noindent since $\eta(e_1(A_1)) = e_2(A_2)$ and $v$ is the $i$th end of $e_1(A_1)$, it follows that $\eta(v)$ contains the $i$th end of $e_2(A_2)$; that is, the $i$th term of $\pi(A_2)$. From 5.6 with $P_1, P_2$ replaced by $Q_1', Q_2'$ respectively, there is a realizable expansion $\eta_0$ of $Q_1'$ in $Q_2'$ such that $\eta_0(e) = \eta(e)$ for all $e \in E(Q_1) \setminus \{e_1(A_1)\}$. In particular, if $e \in E(Q_1')$ and $\eta_0(e) = e_2(A_2')$ for some $A_2' \in \mathscr{L}_2'$ then $e = e_1(A_1')$ for some $A_1' \in \mathscr{L}_1'$; and for each $A_1' \in \mathscr{L}_1'$, $\eta_0(e_1(A_1')) = e_2(A_2')$ and $P_1|A_1'$ is simulated in $P_2|A_2'$ for some $A_2' \in \mathscr{L}_2'$. From the inductive hypothesis applied to $\mathscr{L}_1'$ and $\mathscr{L}_2'$, we deduce that $P_1$ is simulated in $P_2$. This proves 5.7. $\square$

\sectionhead{6. Well-behavedness}

Let $P = (G, \mu, \Delta, \phi)$ be a partial $\Omega$-patchwork, and let $\Omega'$ be a quasi-order with $\Omega \subseteq \Omega'$. By an $\Omega'$-completion of $P$ we mean an $\Omega'$-patchwork $(G, \mu, \Delta, \phi')$ such that $\phi'(e) = \phi(e)$ for each $e \in dom(\phi)$. If $\Omega$ is a well-quasi-order, a class $\mathscr{C}$ of partial $\Omega$-patchworks is \textit{well-behaved} if for every well-quasi-order $\Omega'$ with $\Omega \subseteq \Omega'$ and every countable sequence $P_i'$ ($i = 1, 2, \dots$) of $\Omega'$-completions of members of $\mathscr{C}$ there exist $j > i \ge 1$ such that $P_i'$ is simulated in $P_j'$. (We remark that whether $\mathscr{C}$ is well-behaved depends \textit{prima facie} not only on $\mathscr{C}$, but also on $\Omega$; we leave this dependence implicit. In fact, it is an easy exercise to show that there is no dependence on $\Omega$.)

A partial $\Omega$-patchwork $P = (G, \mu, \Delta, \phi)$ is \textit{rootless} if $\bar{\pi}(G) = \emptyset$. Let $P = (G, \mu, \Delta, \phi)$ be a rootless partial $\Omega$-patchwork, let $e \in dom(\mu) \setminus dom(\phi)$, and let $P' = (G', \mu', \Delta', \phi')$ be the partial $\Omega$-patchwork with $G'^- = G^- \setminus e$, $\pi(G') = \mu(e)$, and $P' = P|G'$. We call $P'$ a \textit{rooting} of $P$.

\textbf{6.1.} \textit{Let $\Omega$ be a well-quasi-order, and let $\mathscr{C}$ be a well-behaved class of partial $\Omega$-patchworks. Let $\mathscr{C}'$ be the class of all rootings of rootless members of $\mathscr{C}$. Then $\mathscr{C}'$ is well-behaved.}

\textbf{Proof.} Let $\Omega'$ be a well-quasi-order with $\Omega \subseteq \Omega'$, and let $Q_i'$ ($i = 1, 2, \dots$) be a countable sequence of $\Omega'$-completions of members of $\mathscr{C}'$. Let $*$ be a new element and let $\Omega''$ be the well-quasi-order with $\Omega' \subseteq \Omega''$ and $E(\Omega'') = E(\Omega') \cup \{*\}$, in which if $x \le *$ or $* \le x$ then $x = *$. For each $i$, let $Q_i' = (G_i', \mu_i', \Delta_i', \psi_i')$ be an $\Omega'$-completion of $P_i' = (G_i', \mu_i', \Delta_i', \phi_i') \in \mathscr{C}'$ and choose $P_i = (G_i, \mu_i, \Delta_i, \phi_i) \in \mathscr{C}$ and $e_i \in dom(\mu_i) \setminus dom(\phi_i)$ such that $\bar{\pi}(G_i) = \emptyset$, $\pi(G_i') = \mu_i(e_i)$, $G_i^- \setminus e_i = G_i'^-$ and $P_i' = P_i|G_i'$. Let $Q_i = (G_i, \mu_i, \Delta_i, \psi_i)$ be the $\Omega''$-completion of $P_i$ where
\begin{align*}
\psi_i(e) &= \psi_i'(e) \quad (e \in E(G_i')), \\
\psi_i(e_i) &= *.
\end{align*}
Since $\mathscr{C}$ is well-behaved, there exist $j > i \ge 1$ such that $Q_i$ is simulated in $Q_j$; let $\eta$ be a realizable expansion of $Q_i$ in $Q_j$. Then $\eta(e_i) = e_j$ since $e_j$ is the only edge $e$ of $G_j$ with $\psi_j(e) = *$; and hence there is a realizable expansion of $Q_i|G_i'$ in $Q_j|G_j'$; that is, of $Q_i'$ in $Q_j'$. This proves 6.1. $\square$
