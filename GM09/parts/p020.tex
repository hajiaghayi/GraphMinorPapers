Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a transaction. If there is a fence $(A, B)$ for $\mathcal{P}$, and an enumeration $\mathcal{P} = \{P_1, \dots, P_p\}$, where $P_i$ has ends $a_i \in A$, $b_i \in B$ ($1 \le i \le p$), such that $(a_p, a_{p-1}, \dots, a_1, b_1)$ is clockwise, and

\hspace*{12pt}(i) $p \ge 2$ and $(a_p, a_{p-1}, \dots, a_2, a_1, b_p, b_{p-1}, \dots, b_2, b_1)$ is clockwise, we say $\mathcal{P}$ is a \textit{crosscap}

\hspace*{12pt}(ii) $p \ge 3$ and $(a_p, a_{p-1}, \dots, a_2, a_1, b_2, b_3, \dots, b_p, b_1)$ is clockwise, we say $\mathcal{P}$ is a \textit{leap}

\hspace*{12pt}(iii) $p \ge 4$ and $(a_p, a_{p-1}, \dots, a_2, a_1, b_2, b_1, b_3, b_4, \dots, b_{p-3}, b_{p-2}, b_p, b_{p-1})$ is clockwise, we say $\mathcal{P}$ is a \textit{doublecross}.
