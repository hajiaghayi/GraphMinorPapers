\sectionhead{3. Matchings and Forests}

Let $\Sigma$ be a surface and let $F, F'$ be proper $I$-arcs in $\Sigma$. We say that $F$ is \textit{similar} to $F'$ if there is a homeomorphism $\alpha \colon \Sigma \to \Sigma$ such that $\alpha(F) = F'$. This is an equivalence relation, and we call the equivalence classes \textit{similarity classes}.
