\sectionhead{7. Another adjustment}

Before we apply 6.3 to Wagner's conjecture, it is convenient to make one further small adjustment to it. We begin with the following lemma. A patchwork $(G, \mu, \Delta)$ or $\Omega$-patchwork $(G, \mu, \Delta, \phi)$ is free if $\Delta(e)$ is free for all $e \in E(G)$.

\textbf{7.1.} \textit{Let $P = (G, \mu, \Delta)$ be a free patchwork and $W \subseteq \bar{\pi}(G)$. Let $K$ be a grouping with $V(K) = \bar{\pi}(G) \setminus W$. Then $K$ is feasible in $P/W$ if and only if $K \cup N_W$ is feasible in $P$.}

\textbf{Proof.} If $K$ is feasible in $P/W$, let
\begin{equation*}
H' = N_{V(G) \setminus W} \cup \bigcup(\delta'_e : e \in E(G))
\end{equation*}
be a realization of $P/W$ such that for distinct $x, y \in \bar{\pi}(G) \setminus W$, $x$ and $y$ are connected in $H$ if and only if they are adjacent in $K$. For each $e \in E(G)$ there exists $\delta_e \in \Delta(e)$ such that the vertices of $\delta_e$ in $W$ are isolated vertices of $\delta_e$ and their removal yields $\delta'_e$. Let
\begin{equation*}
H = N_{V(G)} \cup \bigcup(\delta_e : e \in E(G)).
\end{equation*}
Then for distinct $x \ y \in \bar{\pi}(G)$, $x$ and $y$ are connected in $H$ if and only if they are adjacent in $K \cup N_W$, as required. For the converse, let $K \cup N_W$ be feasible in $P$, and choose a corresponding realization
\begin{equation*}
H = N_{V(G)} \cup \bigcup(\delta_e : e \in E(G)).
\end{equation*}
Since $P$ is free, we may choose $H$ and the $\delta_e$'s such that for each $e \in E(G))$ every vertex of $W$ in $V(\delta_e)$ is an isolated vertex of $\delta_e$. Then $H/W$ is a realization of $P/W$ with the required properties. This proves 7.1. $\square$
