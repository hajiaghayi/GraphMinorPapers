\textit{Proof.} From (7.8) there is a respectful tangle $\mathscr{T}''$ order $\theta - 2k$ in $\Gamma'$, with metric $d''$, such that
\begin{equation*}
d''(a', b') \ge d(a, b) - 4k - 2
\end{equation*}
for all $a, b \in A(\Gamma)$ and $a', b' \in A(\Gamma')$ with $a \subseteq a'$ and $b \subseteq b'$. Let $\mathscr{T}'$ be the set of all members of $\mathscr{T}''$ of order $< \theta - 4k - 2$. Then $\mathscr{T}'$ is respectful tangle of order $\theta - 4k - 2$ in $\Gamma'$, with metric $d'$; and for $a', b' \in A(\Gamma')$,
\begin{equation*}
d'(a', b') = \min(d''(a', b'), \theta - 4k - 2).
\end{equation*}
Thus for $a, b \in A(\Gamma)$ and $a', b' \in A(\Gamma')$ with $a \subseteq a'$ and $b \subseteq b'$,
\begin{align*}
d'(a', b') &= \min(d''(a', b'), \theta - 4k - 2) \\
&\ge \min(d(a, b) - 4k - 2, \theta - 4k - 2) = d(a, b) - 4k - 2
\end{align*}
and so $\mathscr{T}'$ satisfies the first claim of the theorem. Moreover, (i) and (ii) follows from (7.9). For (iii), let $a, b, a', b'$ be as usual; then from (7.7)(i),
\begin{equation*}
d(a, b) \ge \min(d'(a', b'), \theta - 4k - 2) = d'(a', b')
\end{equation*}
and so (iii) holds. $\square$
