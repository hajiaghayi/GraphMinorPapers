\textit{There are mutually disjoint, connected subdrawings $H_1, \dots, H_p$ of $\Gamma$ with $V(H_j) \cap Z = Z_j$ $(1 \le j \le p)$.} (*)

One necessary condition for (*) to hold is the following.

\textit{Topological Feasibility Condition.} There exist mutually disjoint closed discs $D_1, \dots, D_p \subseteq \Sigma$ such that
\begin{equation*}
D_j \cap (\Delta_1 \cup \dots \cup \Delta_t) = Z_j \quad (1 \le j \le p).
\end{equation*}
(We observe that this condition does not depend on $\Gamma$, but only on $\Delta_1, \dots, \Delta_t$ and $Z_1, \dots, Z_p$.) The following was shown in [3, Sections 7 and 8]. Let $\Sigma^*$ be the closure of $\Sigma - (\Delta_1 \cup \dots \cup \Delta_t)$.

\stmt
(3.1) \textit{With notation as above, suppose that either $\Sigma$ is not a sphere or $t \ge 3$. Then there is an integer $N$, depending on $\Sigma$, $t$, $|Z|$ but not on $\Gamma$, such that for every $\Gamma$ and partition $(Z_j \colon 1 \le j \le p)$ of $Z$ the following is true. Suppose that}

\hspace*{12pt}(i) \textit{if $F \subseteq \Sigma^*$ is a $\Gamma$-normal $O$-arc with $|F \cap V(\Gamma)| \le N$ then $F$ bounds a closed disc $\Delta \subseteq \Sigma$ which includes at most one of $\Delta_1, \dots, \Delta_t$,}

\hspace*{12pt}(ii) \textit{for every triple $F_1$, $F_2$, $F_3 \subseteq \Sigma^*$ such that $F_1$, $F_2$ are $\Gamma$-normal $O$-arcs bounding disjoint closed discs in $\Sigma$ which each include exactly one of $\Delta_1, \dots, \Delta_t$, and $F_3$ is a $\Gamma$-normal line with an end in $F_1$ and an end in $F_2$ and no other point in $F_1 \cup F_2$, we have $|(F_1 \cup F_2 \cup F_3) \cap V(\Gamma)| > N$}

\hspace*{12pt}(iii) \textit{for every pair $F_1$, $F_2 \subseteq \Sigma^*$ of $\Gamma$-normal $O$-arcs bounding closed discs $D_1$, $D_2 \subseteq \Sigma$ which each include exactly one of $\Delta_1, \dots, \Delta_t$, and with $|D_1 \cap D_2| = 1$, we have $|(F_1 \cup F_2) \cap V(\Gamma)| > N$}

\hspace*{12pt}(iv) \textit{if $\Sigma$ is a sphere and $t = 3$, and $F_1$, $F_2$, $F_3 \subseteq \Sigma^*$ are $\Gamma$-normal lines which have common ends but are otherwise mutually disjoint, and the $O$-arcs $F_2 \cup F_3$, $F_3 \cup F_1$, $F_1 \cup F_2$ respectively bound closed discs $D_1$, $D_2$, $D_3 \subseteq \Sigma$, say, where $F_i \not\subseteq D_i$ and $\Delta_i \subseteq D_i$ $(i = 1, 2, 3)$, then $|(F_1 \cup F_2 \cup F_3) \cap V(\Gamma)| > N$}

\hspace*{12pt}(v) \textit{for every $\Gamma$-normal $O$-arc $F \subseteq \Sigma^*$ which bounds a closed disc in $\Sigma$ including exactly one of $\Delta_1, \dots, \Delta_t$, ($\Delta_i$ say) we have $|F \cap V(\Gamma)| \ge |\text{bd}(\Delta_i) \cap V(\Gamma)|$, and}

\hspace*{12pt}(vi) \textit{the topological feasibility condition is satisfied.}

\textit{Then} (*) \textit{holds.}
