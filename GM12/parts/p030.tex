\textit{Proof.} Let $K$ be a radial drawing of $\Gamma$, and let $h$ be the atom of $K$ corresponding to $c$. Now either $h = \{v\}$ for some $v \in V(K)$, or $2k \ge 4$ and $h$ is a region of $K$ incident with $\le 4$ edges of $K$; and so in either case there is a closed walk $W_0$ of $K$ of length $\le 2k$ with $h \subseteq \text{ins}(W_0)$, that is, with $c \cap \text{ins}(W_0) \ne \emptyset$. Choose $W_0$ with $\text{ins}(W_0)$ maximal.

If $V(W) \subseteq V(\Gamma)$ then $V(W) = \{v\}$ for some $v \in V(\Gamma)$; and hence $k = 0$ and $\Gamma' = \Gamma$, and the theorem is satisfied taking $\mathscr{T}' = \mathscr{T}$. We may assume, therefore, that there exists $v_0 \in V(W_0) - V(\Gamma)$. Let $r$ be the region of $\Gamma$ with $v_0 \in r$. Let $Z$ be the set of all vertices $v$ of $\Gamma$ such that there is a $\Gamma$-normal line $F$ with $v \in F, F \cap r \ne \emptyset$ and $|F \cap V(\Gamma)| \le k$ (or equivalently, all $v \in V(\Gamma)$ such that there is a path of $K$ between $v$ and $v_0$ with $\le 2k$ edges). By (7.6), there is a 2-cell subdrawing $\Gamma''$ of $\Gamma \setminus Z$, and a respectful tangle $\mathscr{T}''$ of order $\theta - 2k$ in $\Gamma''$, with metric $d''$, such that
\begin{equation*}
d''(a'', b'') \ge d(a, b) - 4k - 2
\end{equation*}
for all $a, b \in A(\Gamma)$ and $a'', b'' \in A(\Gamma'')$ with $a \subseteq a''$ and $b \subseteq b''$, and such that $d(r, f) \le 2k + 1$ for all $f \in E(\Gamma) - E(\Gamma'')$.

We claim that $\Gamma'' \subseteq \Gamma'$. For suppose not. Since $\Gamma''$ has no isolated vertices (because it is 2-cell and has a tangle of order $\ge 2$) there is an edge $e$ of $\Gamma''$ with $e \notin E(\Gamma')$. Since $e \in E(\Gamma) - E(\Gamma')$ it follows from the hypothesis that $d(c, e) \le k$. Since $k < \theta$ (and since $k \ge 2$ if $e = c$) there is a closed walk $W_1$ of $K$ of length $\le 2k$ such that $e \subseteq \text{ins}(W_1)$ and $c \cap \text{ins}(W_1) \ne \emptyset$. Now $\text{ins}(W_0) \cup \text{ins}(W_1) \ne \Sigma$, by [5, Theorem (8.2)], and $\text{ins}(W_0) \nsubseteq \text{ins}(W_1)$ by the maximality of $W_0$. But $bd(\text{ins}(W_0))$ and $bd(\text{ins}(W_1))$ are both arc-wise connected, by [5, Theorem (4.4)], and so by [5, Theorem (7.7)], either $\text{ins}(W_1) \subseteq \text{ins}(W_0)$ or $V(W_1) \cap V(W_0) \ne \emptyset$. Since $e \subseteq \text{ins}(W_1)$, it follows in either case that there is a closed walk $W$ in $K$ of length $\le 4k$ with $v_0 \in V(W)$ and $e \subseteq \text{ins}(W)$. Since $W$ has length $\le 4k$, it follows from the definition of $Z$ that $V(W) \cap V(\Gamma) \subseteq Z$ and hence $V(W) \cap V(\Gamma'') = \emptyset$. Since $\Gamma''$ is connected, $U(\Gamma'') \cap bd(\text{ins}(W)) = \emptyset$, and $e \subseteq U(\Gamma'') \cap \text{ins}(W)$, it follows that $U(\Gamma'') \subseteq \text{ins}(W)$. Now by (1.2), there is an edge $f$ of $\Gamma$ such that $d(r, f) = \theta$. Since $\theta > 2k + 1$, it follows from the choise of $\Gamma''$ that $f \in E(\Gamma'')$, and hence $f \subseteq \text{ins}(W)$. Consequently, $d(r, f) \le 2k < \theta$, a contradiction. This proves that $\Gamma'' \subseteq \Gamma'$.

Let $\mathscr{T}'$ be the tangle of order $\theta - 2k$ induced by $\mathscr{T}''$ in $\Gamma'$. Then $\mathscr{T}'$ is respectful; let its metric be $d'$. Let $a, b \in A(\Gamma)$ and $a', b' \in A(\Gamma')$ with $a \subseteq a'$ and $b \subseteq b'$; and let $a'', b'' \in A(\Gamma'')$ such that $a' \subseteq a''$ and $b' \subseteq b''$. By the choice of $\Gamma''$,
\begin{equation*}
d''(a'', b'') \ge d(a, b) - 4k - 2.
\end{equation*}
But from (7.7)(ii), $d'(a', b') \ge d''(a'', b'')$, and so $\mathscr{T}'$ satisfies the theorem. $\square$
