\stmt
(7.6) \textit{Let $k \ge 0$ be an integer, and let $\mathscr{T}$ be a respectful tangle of order $\theta \ge 2k + 1$ in a 2-cell drawing $\Gamma$ in a connected surface $\Sigma$. Let its metric be $d$. Let $r$ be a region of $\Gamma$, and let $Z$ be the set of all vertices $v$ of $\Gamma$ such that there is a $\Gamma$-normal line $F$ with $v \in F$, $F \cap r \ne \emptyset$ and $|F \cap V(\Gamma)| \le k$. Then there is a 2-cell subdrawing $\Gamma'$ of $\Gamma \setminus Z$, and a respectful tangle $\mathscr{T}'$ of order $\theta - 2k$ in $\Gamma'$, with metric $d'$, such that if $r'$ is the region of $\Gamma'$ with $r \subseteq r'$, then}
\begin{align*}
\text{(i)} \quad & U(\Gamma) \subseteq U(\Gamma') \cup r' \\
\text{(ii)} \quad & d'(a, b) \ge d(a, b) - 4k \text{ for all } a, b \in A(\Gamma) \cap A(\Gamma') \\
\text{(iii)} \quad & d'(a, r') \ge d(a, r) - 2k \text{ for all } a \in A(\Gamma) \cap A(\Gamma'), \text{ and} \\
\text{(iv)} \quad & d(a, r) \le 2k + 1 \text{ for all } a \in A(\Gamma) \text{ with } a \subseteq r'.
\end{align*}
\textit{In particular, if $a, b \in A(\Gamma)$ and $a', b' \in A(\Gamma')$ with $a \subseteq a'$ and $b \subseteq b'$, then $d'(a', b') \ge d(a, b) - 4k - 2$.}

\textit{Proof.} Let us show first that the last claim of the theorem follows from (i)-(iv). Let $\Gamma', \mathscr{T}'$ satisfy (i)-(iv), and let $a, b \in A(\Gamma)$ and $a', b' \in A(\Gamma')$ with $a \subseteq a'$ and $b \subseteq b'$. If $a = a'$ and $b = b'$, then $a, b \in A(\Gamma')$ and the claim follows from (ii). We assume then that $b \ne b'$, and so $b' = r'$ from (i). If $a = a'$ then from (iii),
\begin{equation*}
d'(a, r') \ge d(a, r) - 2k;
\end{equation*}
but from (iv), $d(b, r) \le 2k + 1$, and so
\begin{equation*}
d'(a, r') \ge d(a, r) + d(b, r) - 4k - 1 \ge d(a, b) - 4k - 1
\end{equation*}
as required. Finally, if $a \ne a'$, then $a' = b' = r'$ and from (iv),
\begin{equation*}
d(a, b) \le d(a, r) + d(b, r) \le 2(2k + 1) = d'(a', b') + 4k + 2,
\end{equation*}
as required. Thus, it remains to find $\Gamma'$ and $\mathscr{T}'$ satisfying (i)-(iv).
