\sectionhead{4. Clique Minors}

It is convenient to prove here three applications of (3.2) that we shall need in future papers. To do so we shall need the following.

\stmt
(4.1) \textit{Let $\mathscr{T}$ be a respectful tangle in a 2-cell drawing $\Gamma$ in a connected surface $\Sigma$, with metric $d$. Let $u, v \in V(\Gamma)$, and let $P$ be a path of $\Gamma$ between $u$ and $v$. If $(A, B) \in \mathscr{T}$ and $V(P) \subseteq V(A)$ then $(A, B)$ has order $\ge \frac{1}{2} d(u, v)$.}

\textit{Proof.} By [4, Theorem (2.9)], we may assume that $P \subseteq A$ and, clearly, that $E(\Gamma) \ne \emptyset$. By (1.1), there are circuits $C_1, \dots, C_k$ of a radial drawing $K$ of $\Gamma$, such that $V(C_i) \cap V(\Gamma) \subseteq V(A \cap B)$ ($1 \le i \le k$) and such that every edge of $A$ is a subset of one of $\text{ins}(C_1), \dots, \text{ins}(C_k)$. Now since $U(P) \subseteq \text{ins}(C_1) \cup \dots \cup \text{ins}(C_k)$, we may renumber $C_1, \dots, C_k$ so that $u \in \text{ins}(C_1)$, $v \in \text{ins}(C_j)$ for some $j$, $\text{ins}(C_i) \cap \text{ins}(C_{i+1}) \ne \emptyset$ for all $i$ with $1 \le i < j$, and $\text{ins}(C_i) \cap \text{ins}(C_{i'}) = \emptyset$ for all $i, i'$ with $1 \le i, i' \le j$ and $i' > i + 1$. Then
\begin{equation*}
d(u, v) \le \frac{1}{2} (|E(C_1)| + \dots + |E(C_j)|) = \sum_{1 \le i \le j} |V(C_i) \cap V(\Gamma)|.
\end{equation*}
But no vertex of $\Gamma$ belongs to more than two of $V(C_1), \dots, V(C_j)$, and so
\begin{equation*}
\sum_{1 \le i \le j} |V(C_i) \cap V(\Gamma)| \le 2 |V(C_1 \cup \dots \cup C_j) \cap V(\Gamma)| \le 2 |V(A \cap B)|.
\end{equation*}
The result follows. $\square$

Let $G'$ be a subgraph of a graph $G$, and let $\mathscr{T}'$ be a tangle in $G'$ of order $\theta$. Let $\mathscr{T}$ be the set of all separations $(A, B)$ of $G$ of order $< \theta$ such that $(A \cap G', B \cap G') \in \mathscr{T}'$. Evidently $\mathscr{T}$ is a tangle in $G$ of order $\theta$, and we call it the tangle \textit{induced by $\mathscr{T}'$}.
