\stmt
(7.7) \textit{Let $\mathscr{T}$ be a respectful tangle of order $\theta$ in a 2-cell drawing $\Gamma$ in a connected surface $\Sigma$; let its metric be $d$. Let $\Gamma'$ be a 2-cell subdrawing of $\Gamma$, and let $\mathscr{T}'$ be a respectful tangle of order $\theta' \le \theta$ in $\Gamma'$, with metric $d'$. Then}

\hspace*{12pt}(i) \textit{for $k \ge 0$, if $d'(a', b') \ge d(a, b) - k$ for all $a, b \in A(\Gamma)$ and $a', b' \in A(\Gamma')$ with $a \subseteq a'$ and $b \subseteq b'$, then $d(a, b) \ge \min(d'(a', b'), \theta - k)$ for all such $a, b, a', b'$}

\hspace*{12pt}(ii) \textit{if $\theta = \theta'$ and $\mathscr{T}$ is the tangle in $\Gamma$ induced by $\mathscr{T}'$, then $d(a, b) \ge d'(a', b')$ for all $a, b \in A(\Gamma)$ and $a', b' \in A(\Gamma')$ with $a \subseteq a'$ and $b \subseteq b'$.}

\textit{Proof.} We prove both statements simultaneously. In case (ii), let $k = 0$. We assume that $a, b \in A(\Gamma)$, $a', b' \in A(\Gamma')$, $d(a, b) < d'(a', b') < \theta'$, and $d(a, b) < \theta - k$, and we shall show that neither case (i) nor case (ii) applies. Let $K$ be a radial drawing of $\Gamma$, and let $\text{ins}, \text{ins}'$ be the inside functions of $\mathscr{T}$ and $\mathscr{T}'$. Now $a \ne b$, since $d'(a', b') > 0$ and, hence, $a' \ne b'$. But $d(a, b) < \theta$, and so there is a closed walk $W$ of $K$ of length $2d(a, b)$, such that $a \cap \text{ins}(W)$, $b \cap \text{ins}(W)$ are both non-empty. Let $\phi \colon S^1 \to \Sigma$ ``follow'' $W$ in the natural sense; then $|\phi|$ is $\Gamma$-normal and has length $d(a, b)$ with respect to $\Gamma$, and $\text{ins}(\phi)$ meets both $a$ and $b$. Consequently, $|\phi|$ is $\Gamma'$-normal and has length $\le d(a, b) < \theta'$ with respect to $\Gamma'$. Since $d'(a', b') > d(a, b)$, $\text{ins}'(\phi)$ does not meet both $a'$ and $b'$ by (2.1), and, hence, we may assume that $a \cap \text{ins}'(\phi) = \emptyset$, since $a \subseteq a'$. Consequently $a \cap |\phi| = \emptyset$, and since $a \cap \text{ins}(\phi) \ne \emptyset$, there is an O-arc $F \subseteq |\phi|$ with $a \subseteq \text{ins}(F)$. Now $F$ is $\Gamma$-normal, and $|F \cap V(\Gamma)| \le d(a, b) < \theta'$. Since $a \cap \text{ins}'(F) = \emptyset$, it follows that $\text{ins}(F) \ne \text{ins}'(F)$, and so $\mathscr{T}$ is not induced by $\mathscr{T}'$. Hence the hypothesis of case (ii) does not hold. Choose $c \in A(\Gamma)$ such that $F \cap c \ne \emptyset$. By (1.2), there exists $f \in E(\Gamma)$ such that $d(c, f) = \theta$. Let $c', f' \in A(\Gamma')$ with $c \subseteq c'$ and $f \subseteq f'$. Then $f \notin \text{ins}(F)$, since $c \cap \text{ins}(F) \ne \emptyset$ and $|F \cap V(\Gamma)| < \theta$. Since $\text{ins}(F) \ne \text{ins}'(F)$, it follows that $f \subseteq \text{ins}'(F)$, and so by (2.1),
\begin{equation*}
d'(c', f') \le |F \cap V(\Gamma')| \le d(a, b) < \theta - k = d(c, f) - k.
\end{equation*}
Consequently, the hypothesis of case (i) does not hold, as required. $\square$
