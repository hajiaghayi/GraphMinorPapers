\stmt
(6.1) \textit{Let $S$ be a surface of genus $g > 0$, and let $H$ be a graph which may be embedded in $S$. Then there is a number $k$ with the following property. For every graph $G$ which has no minor isomorphic to $H$ but which can be embedded in $S$, there exists $X \subseteq V(G)$ with $|X| \le k$ such that $G \setminus X$ can be embedded on a surface of genus $<g$.}
