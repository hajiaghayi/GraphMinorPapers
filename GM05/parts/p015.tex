The following extends (6.3) to disconnected graphs.

\stmt
(6.4) \textit{Let $G$ be a graph and let $k>0$ be an integer. Then at least one of the following holds:}

\hspace*{12pt}(i) \textit{there are $k$ disjoint connected subgraphs of $G$, each with at least $4(3^k-1)^{-1}|V(G)|/3$ vertices;}

\hspace*{12pt}(ii) \textit{there is a separation $(V_1, V_2)$ of $G$ such that $|V_1\cap V_2|<k$ and $|V_1 - V_2|, |V_2 - V_1|\le 2|V(G)|/3$.}

\textit{Proof.} Let $G$ have components $G_1,..., G_t$, where $|V(G_1)|\ge |V(G_2)|\ge \cdots \ge |V(G_t)|$. If $|V(G_1)|\le 2|V(G)|/3$ then (ii) is true; indeed, we may choose $V_1, V_2$ in (ii) with $V_1\cap V_2=\emptyset$, as is easily seen. We assume then that $|V(G_1)|>2|V(G)|/3$, and apply (6.3) to $G_1$. If (6.3) (i) holds for $G_1$, then (i) holds for $G$. We assume then that (6.3) (ii) holds for $G_1$, and $X\subseteq V(G_1)$ is such that $|X|<k$ and every component of $G_1\setminus X$ has fewer than $|V(G_1)|/3$ vertices. Now
\begin{equation*}
|V(G_2)|,..., |V(G_t)|\le |V(G)|-|V(G_1)| < |V(G)|/3,
\end{equation*}
and so every component of $G\setminus X$ has fewer than $|V(G)|/3$ vertices. It follows easily that (ii) holds, as required.
