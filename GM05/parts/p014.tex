But $B_i$ is a connected graph, and it does not have two disjoint connected subgraphs both with at least $b_1+1$ vertices, from the lexicographic lastness of $d(\mathcal{B})$. Hence by (6.2) with $w\equiv 1$, there is a vertex $v_i$ of $B_i$ such that every component of $B_i\setminus v_i$ has at most $b_1$ vertices. Let $X=\{v_{i'},..., v_k\}$. We shall show that $X$ satisfies (ii). Let $C$ be a component of $G\setminus X$, and suppose for a contradiction that $|V(C)|\ge |V(G)|/3$. There are two cases.

\textit{Case} 1. $V(C)\cap (V_1\cup\cdots\cup V_{i'-1})\ne \emptyset$.

Now
\begin{equation*}
|V_1\cup\cdots\cup V_{i'-1}| \le b_1 + 3b_1 + \cdots + 3^{i'-2}b_1
\end{equation*}
by the minimality of $i'$, and so
\begin{equation*}
|V_1\cup\cdots\cup V_{i'-1}| \le (3^{i'-1}-1)b_1/2 \le (3^k-1)b_1/6 < |V(G)|/3.
\end{equation*}
Hence $V(C)\cap (V_{i'}\cup\cdots\cup V_k)\ne \emptyset$. But $C$ is connected, $V(C)\cap X=\emptyset$ and $V(G)=V_1\cup\cdots\cup V_k$, and so there exist $u\in V_1\cup\cdots\cup V_{i'-1}$, $u'\in V_{i'}\cup\cdots\cup V_k$ which are adjacent vertices of $C$, and not in $X$. Let $u\in V_j$, $u'\in V_{j'}$ say, where $1\le j\le i'-1$, $i'\le j'\le k$. Let $B$ be the component of $B_{j'}\setminus v_{j'}$ which contains $u'$. Let $B_j'=G|(V(B_j)\cup V(B))$. Then
\begin{equation*}
|V(B_{j'}\setminus V(B))| = b_{j'} - |V(B)| > 3b_{i'-1} - b_1 \ge b_j,
\end{equation*}
and $B_{j'}\setminus V(B)$, $B_j'$ are both connected; thus the set
\begin{equation*}
\{B_{j'}\setminus V(B), B_j'\} \cup \{B_i\colon 1\le i\le k, i\ne j, j'\}
\end{equation*}
contradicts the lexicographic lastness of $d(\mathcal{B})$.

\textit{Case} 2. $V(C)\cap (V_1\cup\cdots\cup V_{i'-1})=\emptyset$.

Hence $V(C)\subseteq (V_{i'}\cup\cdots\cup V_k)-X$. Let us say that $V'\subseteq V(G)$ is a \textit{piece} if for some $i$ ($i'\le i\le k$), $V'$ is the vertex set of a component of $B_i\setminus v_i$. Any two pieces are thus disjoint. Now $V(C)$ is a union of pieces, and
\begin{equation*}
|V(C)|\ge |V(G)|/3 > (3^k-1)b_1/6 \ge b_1
\end{equation*}
