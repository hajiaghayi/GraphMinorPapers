\textit{Case 2. $|N(V(L'))| \ge p$ for some connected subgraph $L'$ of $L$.}

Choose $I \subseteq N(V(L'))$ with $|I| = p$. For each $i \in I$ choose $j_i \in \{1, \dots, n\}$ such that there is a subpath of $B_{j_i}$ joining $A_i$ and $A_h$ for some $h \in V(L')$, with no interior vertex in any of $A_1, \dots, A_m$. Let $P_i$ be such a subpath. Choose a spanning tree of $L'$ with edge-set $E'$ say, and for each $e \in E'$ with ends $i$, $i'$ say, choose $j_e \in \{1, \dots, n\}$ such that there is a subpath of $B_{j_e}$ joining $A_i$ and $A_{i'}$ with no interior vertex in any of $A_1, \dots, A_m$. Let $Q_e$ be such a subpath. Let $C$ be the subgraph of $G$ consisting of the paths $A_i$ ($i \in V(L')$), $P_i$ ($i \in I$) and $Q_e$ ($e \in E'$). Then $C$ is connected, and has a unique vertex in common with each $A_i$ ($i \in I$), and that vertex has valency 1 in $C$. Choose
\begin{equation*}
J \subseteq \{1, \dots, n\} - \{j_i \colon i \in I\} - \{j_e \colon e \in E'\}
\end{equation*}
with $|J| = q$. This is possible since
\begin{equation*}
n - |I| - |E'| \ge n - p - m = q.
\end{equation*}
Then $((A_i \colon i \in I), (B_j \colon j \in J))$ is a ($p, q$)-web with spider $C$. This completes the proof.

(4.5) \textit{For $0 \le k < \delm{\theta_2}\fix{\theta_1}$,\fixnote{fix:4.5}{Here $\phi_k$ and $\psi_k$ are defined only for $0\le k\le\theta_1$ (three lines above), and (4.6) applies (4.5) up to $k=\theta_1$.} if $G \in \mathcal{F}_\theta$ has a ($\phi_k, \psi_k$)-web with $k$ edge-disjoint spiders, then it has a ($\phi_{k+1}, \psi_{k+1}$)-web with $k + 1$ edge-disjoint spiders.}

\textit{Proof.} Let $((A_1, \dots, A_{\phi_k}), (B_1, \dots, B_{\psi_k}))$ be a ($\phi_k, \psi_k$)-web with $k$ edge-disjoint spiders $C_1, \dots, C_k$. Let $G'$ be obtained from $G$ by deleting the edges of $C_1, \dots, C_k$. Then $((A_1, \dots, A_{\phi_k}), (B_1, \dots, B_{\psi_k}))$ is a ($\phi_k, \psi_k$)-web in $G'$. By (4.4) there exists $I \subseteq \{1, \dots, \phi_k\}$ with $|I| = \phi_{k+1}$, and $J \subseteq \{1, \dots, \psi_k\}$ with $|J| = \psi_{k+1}$ such that $((A_i \colon i \in I), (B_j \colon j \in J))$ is a ($\phi_{k+1}, \psi_{k+1}$)-web in $G'$ with a spider $C_{k+1}$. But then this is a web in $G$, and in $G$ it has $k + 1$ edge-disjoint spiders $C_1, \dots, C_{k+1}$.
